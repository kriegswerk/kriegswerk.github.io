About

I create winning designs and teams that excel.

I’m an award-winning multidisciplinary design leader with over 15 years of experience shaping impactful, human-centered experiences at Fortune 500 companies like IBM, Oracle, and Dell. I currently lead creative direction for IBM Quantum, envisioning the future of quantum computing through product, web, and brand design. I build cross-functional design teams and drive large-scale enterprise projects that deliver business value and user satisfaction.

From pioneering the Oracle.com design system to spearheading the creation of IBM Quantum’s brand and immersive experiences, I’ve combined strategic vision with creative excellence, earning recognition from the Red Dot, Webby, and D&AD awards. I thrive on collaborating across disciplines, whether designing immersive installations for IBM Quantum or leading user-centric e-commerce platforms at Dell.

With a background spanning product, brand, experiential, and industrial design, I bring a holistic approach to complex design challenges and the next generation of user experiences.
